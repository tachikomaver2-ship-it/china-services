Shopping购物

Shop smart in China在中国聪明购物

From mega apps to night markets — and the payment setup that makes it all work for foreigners. 从超级 App 到夜市——以及让外国人也能顺畅付款的设置方法。

Where to Shop去哪买

🛒Taobao / Tmall淘宝 / 天猫

  • Everything at low prices; needs a Chinese payment method.万物齐全、价格低，需绑定中国支付方式。
  • Use the app with translation help.配合翻译使用 App。

📦JD.com京东

  • Fast, reliable delivery; good for electronics.配送快且可靠，适合买电子产品。
  • English-friendly interface options.提供较友好的英文界面。

🏬Malls & Markets商场与集市

  • Silk Market (Beijing), night markets, local bazaars.秀水街（北京）、夜市与本地集市。
  • Bargaining is expected at markets.在集市可适当还价。
Key step关键一步

Set Up Payment设置支付方式

China is nearly cashless. As a foreigner you can link an international card to Alipay / WeChat Pay. 中国几乎无现金社会。外国人可将国际银行卡绑定支付宝 / 微信支付。

1

Download the app下载 App

Alipay or WeChat (both have English UI).支付宝或微信（均有英文界面）。

2

Verify passport验证护照

Scan your passport in the "Tour Pass" / international section.在"Tour Pass"/国际版块扫描护照。

3

Link card绑定银行卡

Add Visa / Mastercard / UnionPay international.添加 Visa / Mastercard / 银联国际卡。

4

Pay with QR扫码付款

Show your QR code or scan the merchant's to pay.出示二维码或扫商家码即可付款。

💸Tax Refund for Tourists游客退税

  • Look for "Tax Free" signs in eligible stores.在贴有"退税"标识的商店购物。
  • Request the refund form at checkout with your passport.结账时凭护照索取退税单。
  • Claim at the airport before departure.离境前在机场办理退税。

💡Smart Tips聪明贴士

  • Sizes run smaller — check cm measurements.尺码偏小，注意厘米尺寸。
  • Keep receipts for returns (7–15 days).保留小票以便退换（7–15 天）。
  • Compare on platforms before big buys.大额消费前多平台比价。

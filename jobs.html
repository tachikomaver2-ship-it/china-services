Legal first合法优先

Work Visa (Z) in 4 Steps工作签证（Z 签）四步

1

Get a job offer拿到录用

From a company licensed to hire foreigners.来自有聘外资质的公司。

2

Work permit notice工作许可通知

Employer applies via the local labor bureau.雇主向当地人社局申请。

3

Apply for Z visa申请 Z 签

At a Chinese embassy/consulate in your country.在所在国中国使领馆办理。

4

Residence permit居留许可

Convert to a residence permit within 30 days of arrival.入境 30 天内换发居留许可。

📝Résumé Tips简历建议

  • Keep it 1–2 pages, clear and factual.1–2 页，清晰务实。
  • Highlight language skills & overseas experience.突出语言能力与海外经历。
  • Provide a Chinese translation if possible.尽量附中文翻译。
